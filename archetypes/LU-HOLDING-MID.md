## AI surface profile

**Fund analytics or portfolio reporting AI** — third-party platform providing risk analysis, performance attribution, or NAV-adjacent reporting with AI-generated outputs. Outputs are used in board reporting and investor communications. Human review: present at management level but typically not documented as a formal check on AI-generated figures or narratives.

**Document automation** — AI-assisted drafting of fund reports, investor letters, regulatory filings, and compliance documentation. Third-party SaaS tool. Content generated by the tool may be used in regulated communications without disclosure of AI assistance.

---

## Regulatory overlay (Luxembourg)

**EU AI Act:** Full applicability. AI-generated content used in investor communications or regulatory filings may implicate transparency obligations if the recipient could reasonably be unaware that the content was AI-generated. Classification analysis required for any AI system that influences significant decisions on individual investors.

**GDPR:** Investor data processing obligations. The entity is data controller for investor personal data. Data processing agreements must cover all AI tools that access investor records. Third-party SaaS document automation tools that process investor names, financial data, or identity information require compliant DPAs.

**CSSF:** Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier. Active oversight of fund operations, governance, and risk management. Expects operational resilience, auditability, and human accountability for decisions affecting fund performance and investor interests. AI-generated board reporting not reviewed by a named responsible individual creates an accountability gap.

**CNPD:** Luxembourg data protection authority. Active enforcement posture. Investor data processed through third-party AI tools without adequate DPAs or transfer safeguards is an enforcement-grade risk.

**ESG overlay:** Where the holding structure has ESG or sustainable finance reporting obligations (SFDR, taxonomy alignment), AI-assisted data aggregation or narrative generation creates additional auditability and accuracy requirements.

---

## Structural exposure profile

**Board reporting exposure:** AI-generated outputs used in investor communications create a liability surface if outputs contain inaccuracies, are not disclosed as AI-assisted, or lack documented human review. Fund managers owe investors accuracy and transparency obligations. AI-generated performance narratives not reviewed by a named individual create accountability voids.

**Vendor lock-in:** Fund analytics platforms are typically multi-year contracts with limited auditability of underlying model behavior. Model updates by the vendor that change output characteristics may not trigger notification obligations under standard contract terms. Reversibility is low — platform switching requires parallel validation of historical figures.

**Investor data scope:** Document automation tools that draft investor letters likely process investor personal data (names, account references, financial positions). DPAs covering these specific AI processing operations are absent at archetype level.

**Regulatory filing exposure:** AI-assisted drafting of regulatory filings (CSSF reports, SFDR disclosures) creates a specific risk if the AI-generated text contains errors, mischaracterizations, or non-compliant statements. Attribution of responsibility for filed content requires documented human review.

**Governance gap:** No AI disclosure policy for investor communications. No documented human oversight procedure for AI-generated fund reports. No formal check on AI-generated figures before board presentation. No incident procedure covering AI output errors in regulated communications.

---

## Archetype notes

This archetype fits Luxembourg boutique law firms advising fund management structures, family offices, and holding companies. The combination of CSSF oversight, investor data obligations, and board-level accountability creates a tighter regulatory exposure profile than a standard corporate client. The AI-generated investor communication risk is the clearest law-firm engagement lever — it is bounded, document-level, and creates direct legal exposure for named individuals in management roles.
