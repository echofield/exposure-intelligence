# Exposure Intelligence — Commercial Offer Architecture

> Layer: commercial
> Version: 1.0
> Audience: partner sales conversations, pricing decisions, partner onboarding

---

## 1. Offer Architecture

Four tiers. Across four products. One distribution model.

All pricing is hypothesis-grade until validated in partner conversations. Do not present any tier as a fixed rate before the first commercial close.

**The logic:**

Entry proves the product. Standard is the practice. Premium is the deal. Flagship is the program.

No partner skips tiers arbitrarily. A partner that starts at entry has the context to move to standard. A flagship engagement is won by the cumulative trust of prior work — not by a cold first meeting.

---

## 2. Entry Tier

**What it is:** A pilot evidence pack delivered within a partner's existing client relationship. Used to test repeatability and prove value before a commercial commitment.

**Who buys it:** A boutique law firm partner with one client who has deployed AI tools and has an immediate need for a structured evidence base — for a board presentation, an employment audit, a data protection review, or a question they cannot answer without a technical evidence layer.

**Deliverable:** AI Exposure Evidence Pack — single archetype, 9-section grammar, diagnostic format.

**Delivery window:** 10 to 15 business days.

**Price hypothesis:** €3k to €7.5k

**Positioning:**

This is not the commercial rate. It is a proof-of-concept at a pilot price. The partner does not need to run a procurement process to approve it. It fits within a discretionary budget.

The partner presents it to the client as an enhanced due diligence instrument, not a new service line. The client does not need to understand Exposure Intelligence as a category. They receive a premium evidence artifact under the firm's letterhead.

**Meeting test:** "Would you bill a structured technical evidence artifact under your name to one existing client in the next 90 days, for between €3k and €7.5k?"

If yes: proceed to delivery.
If no: use this partner for language validation, not distribution — for now.

## 3. Standard Tier

The working product. What most partners will bill most often.

### Standard — AI Exposure Evidence Pack

**Who buys it:** A law firm advising a company that has deployed AI tools and needs a structured evidence base for compliance advisory, board presentation, employment review, or vendor contract audit.

**Deliverable:** Full AI Exposure Evidence Pack — 9-section grammar, complete evidence gap analysis, escalation triggers, remediation pathways, attorney-ready appendix.

**Price hypothesis:** €8k to €20k

**Positioning:** The partner bills this as a structured technical due diligence instrument, not a consulting report. The price reflects the depth of the evidence pack and the partner's time in interpreting it for the client.

---

### Standard — Sovereign Procurement Dossier (Red-Flag Scan)

**Who buys it:** A law firm advising a B2B SaaS or AI-enabled services vendor whose deals are stalling at legal, DPO, or procurement review with regulated buyers.

**Deliverable:** Procurement blocker map, top 10 evidence gaps, buyer objection responses for the most critical procurement gates, remediation priority list.

**Delivery window:** 5 to 7 business days.

**Price hypothesis:** €25k to €50k

**Positioning:** The partner frames this as a procurement readiness diagnostic. The client gets a structured answer to the question: "Why is this deal stalling and what do we need to fix it?"

---

### Standard — Construction Procurement Dossier (Diagnostic)

**Who buys it:** A French public procurement boutique or construction law firm advising a mid-market contractor competing for public tenders with structured award criteria.

**Deliverable:** Evidence architecture assessment, gap map against procurement award criteria, top priority remediation actions, bid narrative structure review.

**Price hypothesis:** €15k to €35k

**Positioning:** The partner frames this as a tender evidence diagnostic. The client gets a structured view of where their evidence is weak and what must be built before the next bid cycle.

---

### Standard — PE AI Dependency Diligence Pack (Red-Flag Scan)

**Who buys it:** M&A counsel or a PE operating partner running diligence on a European SaaS target with meaningful AI dependency.

**Deliverable:** Critical dependency map, top seven deal-killers assessed, customer promise gap flags, priority evidence requests for the data room.

**Delivery window:** 5 to 7 business days.

**Price hypothesis:** €15k to €35k

**Positioning:** The partner frames this as a pre-LOI or post-LOI diligence accelerator. The client gets structured deal intelligence before committing to full diligence spend.

## 4. Premium Tier

Extended scope, board-grade depth, or full deal-grade artifact.

### Premium — Sovereign Procurement Dossier (Full)

**Who buys it:** A B2B SaaS vendor with a serious regulated-market access program — public sector, financial sector, or health. The vendor's board has committed to sovereign procurement as a revenue channel.

**Deliverable:** Complete dossier — procurement gate map, sovereignty control matrix, DORA reversibility architecture, AI Act role matrix, intended-use alignment, contract architecture gap analysis, buyer objection map for three buyer categories, full remediation roadmap (10/30/60/90 days), procurement response pack, board summary.

**Price hypothesis:** €75k to €150k

**Positioning:** The partner frames this as market-access structuring. The client is not buying a compliance report. They are buying the evidence architecture that opens a revenue channel they currently cannot access.

---

### Premium — Construction Procurement Dossier (Board-Ready)

**Who buys it:** A construction group planning a multi-year tender program for public works, infrastructure, energy, or health facilities. Board-level commitment to evidence-backed tendering.

**Deliverable:** Full evidence architecture, sustainability and traceability documentation framework, award criteria alignment analysis, tender narrative structure, contract clause library for the sector, multi-bid application plan.

**Price hypothesis:** €40k to €90k

**Positioning:** The partner frames this as standing tender evidence advisory. The client gets the infrastructure to win tenders systematically, not one-off.

---

### Premium — PE AI Dependency Diligence Pack (Single Target, Full Scope)

**Who buys it:** A PE sponsor or M&A advisory team running full AI dependency diligence on a specific European SaaS or AI-enabled target.

**Deliverable:** Complete dossier — AI dependency concentration map, model substitution risk analysis, data-flow opacity assessment, customer data exposure analysis, prompt and output retention analysis, sub-processor dependency map, IP provenance review, contract architecture mismatch matrix, reversibility and migration feasibility assessment, valuation fragility analysis, post-acquisition integration risk, buyer objection maps for sponsor / acquirer / counsel / lender, evidence request list across three priority tiers, remediation roadmap (30/60/100 days), board summary.

**Price hypothesis:** €40k to €100k

**Positioning:** The partner frames this as deal intelligence, not AI governance. The buyer is paying to find hidden exposure before signing and to identify deal-term levers — price, escrow, warranty, and closing conditions.

## 5. Flagship Tier

Program-level. Multi-company or portfolio scope. Transaction-critical.

The two flagship premium dossiers carry the full analytical depth required for regulated procurement programs, PE portfolio-level AI dependency review, and DORA readiness sprints.

### Flagship — Sovereign Procurement Dossier (DORA Sprint or PE Portfolio)

**Who buys it:** A PE-backed software group, a strategic acquirer, or a vendor group preparing for regulated-market scale. Or a PE fund reviewing sovereign procurement readiness across its portfolio.

**Deliverable:** Full dossier architecture applied across multiple targets or buyer verticals. Includes portfolio-level blocker comparison, standard contract library for the regulated buyer category, deal-desk controls, and partner training on buyer objection handling.

**Price hypothesis:**
- DORA / public-sector sprint: €100k to €200k
- PE portfolio (3-5 companies): €150k to €300k+

**Positioning:** The partner is not billing a report. They are running a market-access program. The client is buying the operational infrastructure to win regulated procurement at scale.

---

### Flagship — PE AI Dependency Diligence Pack (Portfolio)

**Who buys it:** A PE fund or operating partner reviewing AI dependency across 3 to 5 portfolio companies, or a strategic acquirer building an AI dependency diligence protocol for a recurring M&A program.

**Deliverable:** Repeatable diligence review applied across multiple targets. Portfolio-level dependency comparison, concentration risk map, shared remediation roadmap, lender and insurer AI dependency addendum.

**Price hypothesis:**
- Portfolio review (3-5 targets): €150k to €350k+
- Lender or insurer addendum: €40k to €100k

**Positioning:** The partner has become the fund's standing AI dependency diligence advisor. The relationship recurs across every portfolio company and every new acquisition.

The Construction Procurement Dossier has no entry tier. A diagnostic (standard) is already the minimum viable engagement for a serious public procurement practice.

## 7. Progression Logic

| Partner starting point | First product | Natural second product | Flagship path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corporate boutique with AI-active clients | Entry: Evidence Pack | Standard: Evidence Pack (additional clients) | Standard: PE Diligence if M&A mandate arrives |
| Digital law or tech procurement firm | Standard: Sovereign Dossier (red-flag) | Premium: Sovereign Dossier (full) | Flagship: DORA sprint or PE portfolio |
| Public procurement boutique (France) | Standard: Construction Dossier (diagnostic) | Premium: Construction Dossier (board-ready) | Flagship: Multi-tender redesign program |
| M&A counsel or PE advisory firm | Standard: PE Diligence (red-flag) | Premium: PE Diligence (single target, full) | Flagship: PE portfolio review |

### Tier progression test

A partner ready to move from entry to standard has demonstrated: the artifact earns the fee, the evidence gaps generate attorney follow-on work, and the client understands why the artifact is not replaceable by generic frameworks.

A partner ready to move from standard to premium has demonstrated: the standard artifact created scope the client wants to expand, and the partner is comfortable framing the premium product as market-access structuring or deal-grade advisory rather than enhanced documentation.

## 8. Partner Revenue Architecture

| Partner type | Year 1 hypothesis | Year 2 hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate boutique (Tier 1 anchor) | 3–5 evidence packs at standard tier: €25k–100k | Add PE diligence mandate if M&A client emerges: +€40k–100k |
| Digital law or tech procurement firm (Tier 2 anchor) | 1–2 sovereign procurement dossiers at standard or premium: €75k–300k | DORA sprint for one PE portfolio client: +€100k–200k |
| Public procurement boutique (Tier 2 anchor) | 1–2 construction dossiers at standard: €30k–70k | Premium board-ready for anchor client: +€40k–90k |
| M&A counsel or PE advisory firm (Tier 3 anchor) | 2–3 PE diligence red-flag scans: €30k–100k | One full-scope diligence + first portfolio entry: +€100k–200k |

**Revenue floor for a single committed partner relationship:** €25k to €100k in year 1.

**Revenue ceiling across a portfolio-level flagship engagement:** €300k+ for a single fund client.

The partner's economics are strong at every tier because the artifact is produced by the engine, not by partner hours. Partner time goes into client communication, interpretation, and follow-on advisory — all billable under their existing model.
