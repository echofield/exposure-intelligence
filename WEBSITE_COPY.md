## Homepage Rules

These rules govern all homepage implementations. Do not violate without explicit written approval.

- No regulatory article citations on any page element.
- No engine or methodology description visible to visitors.
- No free reports, webinars, or whitepapers offered.
- No use of "AI governance" or "compliance" as primary framing.
- No direct address to the entity — the partner is always in the middle on primary pages.
- No customer logos or case study references until first commercial closes are publicly validated.
- No pricing tables — price anchors with "from" framing only.
- No claim to certify, audit, or issue legal opinions.
- No use of the terms "robust," "comprehensive," "seamless," "holistic," "leveraging," "streamline," "cutting-edge," or "game-changing."
